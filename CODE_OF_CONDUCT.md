# Code of conduct

Pokemontology welcomes constructive technical and research participation.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, protect private information, and respect source licenses and third-party rights. Do not pressure others to publish private data, leaked material, credentials, or copyrighted assets.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. This concise policy is project-authored and is not presented as adoption of an external code-of-conduct template.
